Meet Jordan

About Jordan Ellis, Charleston Real Estate Agent

More than a decade of helping neighbors buy and sell across the Lowcountry, built on honest advice and a builder's eye for detail.

My Story

From Job Sites to Closing Tables

I was born at Roper Hospital and raised on James Island, where summer meant crabbing off the dock and dodging afternoon thunderstorms. My family has called the Lowcountry home for four generations, and I have never wanted to live anywhere else.

After college at the College of Charleston, I spent eight years working for a residential builder in Mount Pleasant. I learned how homes go together and why some age gracefully in salt air while others struggle. That experience still shapes how I walk through every house with my clients.

I earned my South Carolina real estate license in 2012. Since then I have helped first-time buyers, growing families, retirees and relocating professionals find their place here, and I have helped longtime owners sell homes their families held for decades.

Beyond Real Estate

Life in the Lowcountry

When I am not showing homes, you will find me on the water with my family, usually somewhere between Shem Creek and the Morris Island lighthouse. Saturday mornings start at the farmers market in Marion Square, and Sunday evenings often end on the Folly Beach pier.

Working Together

Questions Clients Ask Me

Do you work with both buyers and sellers?

Yes. About half of my clients are buying and half are selling, and many do both at once. I can help you time a sale and a purchase so you are not paying two mortgages or moving twice.

Which areas do you cover?

I work throughout greater Charleston, including the peninsula, Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, James Island, Johns Island, Daniel Island, the beach communities, and Summerville. If you are looking a bit farther out, ask and I will tell you honestly whether I am the right fit.

Can you help if I am moving from out of state?

Absolutely. I give live video tours and connect you with lenders and insurance agents who understand coastal properties. Many relocating clients see a home in person only once before closing.

What does it cost to work with you as a buyer?

We will talk through compensation openly at our first meeting and put it in writing in a buyer agreement before we tour homes. In many cases the seller contributes toward buyer agent compensation, and I will explain how that works for each home you consider.

How quickly can we get started?

Usually within a day or two. A short call is the best first step so I can understand your timing and the kind of home you have in mind.
