Preparing to List

Lowcountry Staging Tips

A few targeted updates can make a big difference in how your home photographs and shows.

  • Pressure wash brick, siding, porches, and walkways. Salt air and humidity leave a film that photos pick up.
  • Style the porch or piazza. Buyers here picture themselves outside, so give them a reason to.
  • Trim back live oaks and shrubs that block windows or touch the roof.
  • Gather documents for roof age and HVAC service. Buyers ask, and having answers builds trust.
  • Edit each room down to its best furniture so the space feels open and calm.
Know Your Numbers

What It Costs to Sell in South Carolina

Understanding your expenses up front makes it easier to evaluate offers. Here are the costs most Charleston sellers should plan for.

Real Estate Commission

Commission is negotiable and is agreed upon in your listing agreement. We will talk through it openly at our first meeting, including how buyer agent compensation is handled.

Deed Recording Fee

South Carolina charges a deed recording fee, often called deed stamps, of $3.70 per $1,000 of the sale price. By custom the seller pays it, although the contract can say otherwise.

Mortgage Payoff and Prorated Taxes

Your remaining loan balance is paid at closing, and property taxes are prorated between you and the buyer based on the closing date.

Attorney Fees and Concessions

Sellers usually pay a fee for deed preparation, and some negotiations include a credit toward the buyer's closing costs or repairs. I will estimate your net proceeds for every offer so you can compare them side by side.

Seller FAQs

Common Questions From Sellers

When is the best time to sell in Charleston?

Spring is traditionally the busiest season, but well-priced homes sell year-round here thanks to steady relocation demand. The right time depends on your next move and how ready your home is to show.

How do you decide on a list price?

I look at recent sales of similar homes and the current competition. Then I adjust for condition, updates, lot, elevation, and flood zone. You will see every comparable I use and why.

Should I make repairs before listing?

Some repairs pay off and some do not. Fixing obvious issues such as leaks or dated paint usually helps. Major remodels rarely return their full cost. I will help you prioritize based on your budget and timing.

Do I need to disclose flooding?

South Carolina sellers complete a disclosure statement covering known material issues, including flooding and water intrusion. Being upfront protects you and builds trust with buyers. Your attorney can answer legal questions about specific situations.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on price point and location. Well-prepared homes priced correctly often go under contract within the first few weeks. After that, closing typically takes 30 to 45 days.
